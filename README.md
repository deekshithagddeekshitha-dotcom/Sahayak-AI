Sahayak AI is an AI-powered citizen assistance system designed to bridge the gap between Karnataka's government services and the people who need them. It addresses challenges such as limited awareness of government schemes, difficulty understanding eligibility criteria, confusion about required documents, and complex application procedures. The system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), LangGraph, FAISS, and embeddings to retrieve relevant information from a predefined knowledge base and deliver clear, context-aware responses through an interactive Gradio chatbot. Citizens can enquire about government schemes, student scholarships, farmer welfare programmes, housing assistance, and other public services without manually searching through multiple information sources. Its intelligent routing workflow directs questions through appropriate processing steps to provide relevant answers, while a fallback mechanism attempts an alternative language model if the primary model fails. By transforming complex government information into accessible conversational responses, Sahayak AI aims to improve public awareness, reduce information barriers, and simplify access to essential services. Future enhancements may include verified Karnataka government documents, Kannada-language support, regularly updated scheme information, and multilingual assistance to serve a wider population. Overall, Sahayak AI demonstrates how artificial intelligence and information retrieval can support accessible, efficient, and citizen-focused digital governance in Karnataka.
